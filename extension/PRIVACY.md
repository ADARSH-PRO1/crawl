# bizfy Scraper: Privacy Policy

_Effective: 31 October 2026. Publisher: Bizfy Labs. Contact: [add a contact email before publishing]._

**Short version: bizfy Scraper does not collect, send or sell any data. Everything it reads stays inside your browser.**

## What the extension does
You open a web page, click the extension's icon, and it shows the main list on that page as a table you can copy or download as CSV or JSON.

## What it reads, and when
- It reads **only the page you are looking at, and only after you click the icon**.
- If you choose **"Click next page"** or **"Scrape each URL"**, the browser asks you to allow access to **that one site**. Only then does the extension open further pages of that same site, in tabs of your own browser, with your own login if you are signed in. It never opens pages on a different site.
- It does not log in for you, fill in forms, solve CAPTCHAs or get around bot checks or login walls. A page that looks like one is skipped and reported.

## What it stores
Stored in your browser's own extension storage, on your device only:
- **Templates** you choose to save: a site name, a CSS selector for the list, the load-more choice and the column names you kept. No page content is saved.
- **Settings**: the Pages and Max URLs numbers you last used.

You can delete a template in the extension at any time, and removing the extension deletes all of it. **Export templates** and **Import templates** read and write a file on your device that you pick.

## What it never does
- It makes **no network requests of its own**: no servers, no analytics, no crash reports, no advertising, no accounts.
- It loads **no remote code**.
- It does not use cookies, browsing history, bookmarks or any data from other tabs.
- It does not sell or share data, because it has none.

## Permissions, in plain words
| Permission | Why |
|---|---|
| `activeTab` | Read the page you clicked the icon on. |
| `scripting` | Put the reading script into that page. |
| `storage` | Remember your templates and settings on your device. |
| Optional access to one site | Only when you pick "Click next page" or "Scrape each URL", so it can open more pages of that site. Asked for each site, never granted in advance. |

## Changes
If this policy changes, the new version will be published here with a new effective date.
